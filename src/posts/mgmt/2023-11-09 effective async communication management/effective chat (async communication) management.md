    <h4>Async Management Rules</h4>

    1️⃣ **Turn Sync to Async**: Avoid interrupting your workflow by switching to asynchronous communication.

    2️⃣ **Minimize Notifications**: Reduce distractions by limiting the number of notifications you receive.

    3️⃣ **Set a D'n'D Schedule**: Disable notifications during focused work hours to maintain productivity.

  </div>

  <div class="col-md">

    <h4>Async Content Rules</h4>  

    1️⃣ **Provide Context**: Ensure your messages include all necessary information and give the other party time to respond comprehensively.

    2️⃣ **Use Formatting Features**: Make your messages more structured and clear.

    3️⃣ **Centralize Communication**: Keep specific discussions in designated channels or threads for easier follow-up during async communication.

    4️⃣ **Transform Communication into a Long-Living Document**: Turn your discussions into valuable documentation for long-term reference.

  </div>
